Privacy Policy

Last updated 3 October 2026

Mockup wording. AurumX's final text replaces this before launch; the structure and design are what this page shows.

What we collect

  • Your name, email address and phone number.
  • Your broker account number, so we can verify your membership.
  • Basic usage data from the dashboard, such as when a key is used.

Why we collect it

  • To check your application and issue your access key.
  • To email you your key and important notices about your access.
  • To keep the service secure and stop keys being shared.

Who sees it

Only AurumX staff who review applications. We use an email provider to send your messages. We do not sell your data and we do not pass it to advertisers.

How long we keep it

For as long as your membership is active and for a reasonable period afterwards so we can handle renewals and disputes. You can ask us to delete it.

Your choices
