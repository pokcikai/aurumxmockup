Customer Support

Last updated 3 October 2026

Mockup wording. AurumX's final text replaces this before launch; the structure and design are what this page shows.

Contact us

Email support@aurumx.example. We reply within 1 business day, and usually much sooner.

Include your registered email and, if it is about your account, your broker account number. That is normally enough for us to find your application without asking for anything sensitive.

Common questions

  • I applied but have no key yet. Every application is checked against the broker's partner report first. This usually takes one working day. Check spam, then email us.
  • My key stopped working. Keys expire at the end of the period you joined for. Your expiry date is shown in the Account tab.
  • I lost my key. The log-in page can send it to the email you registered with.
  • I want to use my key on another device. That is fine. A key is for one person, not one device.

Renewing

Open the membership page and apply again with your new deposit. Days are added to what is left on your key.
