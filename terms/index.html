What AurumX provides

AurumX publishes structured XAUUSD trade setups with an entry, take-profit levels and a stop loss, and a member dashboard to read them. It is an information service.

Not financial advice

Nothing on AurumX is investment advice or a recommendation to trade. You decide whether to place any setup, and you place it yourself. AurumX does not trade on your behalf and never has access to your trading account.

Risk

Trading leveraged products such as gold involves a high risk of loss. Setups can be stopped out. Past results are not a promise of future ones. Never trade money you cannot afford to lose.

Membership and the partner broker

Membership is available to clients who open a new live account with AurumX's partner broker through the AurumX partner link and deposit the amount for their chosen tier. We confirm this against the broker's partner report before issuing an access key. If an application does not match the report, we may decline it or shorten the access period.

Access keys
